# Numainda (نمائندہ)

## Description
AI Customer Service Representative (CSR) agent providing customer support in regional languages such as Sindhi, Balochi, Punjabi, Pashto, Seraiki along with English and Urdu.

## Problem
- Peak hour unavailability of CSRs
- 24/7 availability is costly and difficult to manage
- Lack of regional language support
- Outdated, interactive voice response (IVR) menus
- Dedicated CSRs for different languages
- Human CSRs get tired emotionally/physically
- Missed insights due to manual call listening
- Training bottlenecks when information gets updated
- Delayed follow-ups after customer inquiries
- Manual data lookup and processing is time-consuming

## Solution
- Scalable AI CSR agents
- 24/7 availability of AI CSR agents
- Regional language support (Sindhi, Pashto, Punjabi, Balochi)
- Instant connection with AI CSR agents
- Single, unified multilingual AI CSR agents
- Fatigue-free, consistent conversational quality
- 100% automated call transcription and analytics
- Instant, centralized knowledge base updates
- Automated, immediate outbound routines
- Real-time API integration for instant resolution

## Customers
- Telecommunications (e.g., Jazz, Zong)
- Banking & Fintech (e.g., EasyPaisa, HBL)
- E-commerce stores
- BPOs & Call Centers
- Government Services & Agri-tech

## Users
